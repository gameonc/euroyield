# Reusable Script & Template Library

Copy-paste starting points for every funnel stage. **These are scaffolds, not final copy** —
swap every `[BRACKET]` for the real business, and rewrite so it sounds like a human, not a
template. Anchor the message in the prospect's problem (A-I-M), keep it short, one ask per touch.
Run everything past `compliance.md` before sending (opt-outs, consent, DNC), and against the
relevant `profiles/` file for the industry's angle and objections.

Legend: `[NAME]` prospect · `[YOU]`/`[COMPANY]` you · `[PROBLEM]` their pain · `[OUTCOME]` a
specific result · `[PROOF]` a name/number/case · `[CTA]` the one ask.

---

## Cold email (3–5 touch sequence)

**Touch 1 — problem + proof**
- Subject: `[PROBLEM] at [Company]?` or `quick question`
> Hi [NAME], noticed [specific observation about their company/trigger]. Teams like [PROOF]
> usually deal with [PROBLEM] — we helped them [OUTCOME]. Worth a quick look to see if the same
> applies to [Company]? [CTA: open to 15 min Thursday?]

**Touch 2 (Day ~3) — different angle / proof**
> Hi [NAME], one more thought on [PROBLEM]: [specific insight or stat]. [PROOF] cut theirs by
> [number]. Happy to share how — 15 minutes? Reply "no" and I'll stop.

**Touch 3 (Day ~7) — social proof / resource**
> Hi [NAME], put together a short [teardown/benchmark] on [PROBLEM] for [their segment] — want me
> to send it over? No call required.

**Touch 4 (Day ~12) — breakup**
> Hi [NAME], haven't heard back so I'll close your file. If [PROBLEM] becomes a priority, I'm one
> reply away. All the best — [YOU].

## Cold call

**Opener (permission-based pattern interrupt)**
> "Hi [NAME], it's [YOU] with [COMPANY] — I'll be honest, this is a cold call. Can I have 30
> seconds to tell you why I called, and then you can tell me to get lost?"  *(pause for yes)*

**Reason (their-problem-framed)**
> "We work with [their segment] on [PROBLEM]. Companies like [PROOF] were dealing with [pain], and
> we helped them [OUTCOME]. I don't know if that's a priority for you — worth a quick conversation?"

**Brush-off responses**
- *"Not interested":* "Totally fair — most people say that before they've heard what we do. Can I
  ask: is [PROBLEM] something you've already solved, or just not on the radar right now?"
- *"Send me an email":* "Happy to — so I send something useful and not spam, what's the one thing
  about [PROBLEM] worth addressing? … Great, and let's hold 15 min next week so it's not just
  another email in the pile?"
- *"We're all set / have a vendor":* "Good — most of our clients did too. They switched because
  [differentiator]. Worth a 15-min benchmark to see if you're leaving anything on the table?"

**Voicemail (keep <20 sec)**
> "Hi [NAME], [YOU] at [COMPANY] — reason for my call is [PROBLEM] at [Company]. We helped [PROOF]
> get [OUTCOME]. I'll follow up by email; or call me back at [number]. Thanks."

## LinkedIn / social

**Connect note**
> Hi [NAME] — I work with [their segment] on [PROBLEM]. No pitch, just like to connect with people
> in [industry].

**First message (after accept)**
> Thanks for connecting, [NAME]. Curious how [Company] handles [PROBLEM] these days — we've seen
> [insight]. Open to swapping notes?

## SMS (WARM / consented only — see compliance.md)

**Post-inquiry speed-to-lead text**
> Hi [NAME], it's [YOU] at [COMPANY] — got your inquiry about [TOPIC]. Is now a good time for a
> quick call, or should I try later today? (Reply STOP to opt out.)

**Follow-up nudge**
> Hi [NAME], still happy to help with [PROBLEM] whenever timing's right. Want me to hold a slot
> Thursday? (Reply STOP to opt out.)

## Discovery questions (pick 5–7, listen more than you talk)

- "Walk me through how you handle [AREA] today."
- "What made you take this call / look into this now?" *(the trigger)*
- "What's [PROBLEM] actually costing you — time, money, or risk?" *(impact)*
- "If we fixed that, what changes for you?" *(motivation)*
- "Who else weighs in on a decision like this?" *(influence)*
- "What would need to be true for this to be an easy yes?"
- "What's your timeline to have this solved?"

---

## Objection responses (acknowledge → isolate → respond → confirm)

- **Price:** "Fair. Is it the price itself, or that the value isn't clear yet? … You said [PROBLEM]
  costs you [impact]; here's how the math works out against that."
- **Timing / "not now":** "Makes sense. What would change between now and [date] that makes it the
  right time? … Let's put a hold on [date] so it doesn't slip."
- **"Need to think about it":** "Of course — what specifically do you want to think through? Often
  it's [X or Y] — happy to clear that up now."
- **Partner/committee:** "Totally — who else should be in the room? Let's get them the same info so
  you're not stuck selling it internally."
- **Incumbent / "we have someone":** "Good — what would have to be missing for you to look
  elsewhere? … That's exactly where clients switched to us."
- **Trust/skepticism:** "Reasonable to be skeptical. Here's [PROOF]; and we can start with
  [low-risk first step] so you're not betting the farm."

## Warm follow-up & lifecycle

**Referral ask (at a positive moment — see relational-acquisition.md)**
> [NAME], glad [OUTCOME] is working out. Who else do you know dealing with [PROBLEM] who'd want the
> same result? Happy to take great care of them.

**Reactivation (dead / old closed-lost)**
> Hi [NAME], it's been a while. Since we last talked we've [new thing / price / capacity]. Given
> [PROBLEM], worth a fresh look? No pressure either way.

**Post-close handoff (to the customer)**
> [NAME], excited to get started. Here's what happens next: [step 1 by date], [step 2], and your
> point of contact is [OWNER]. First milestone we're aiming for: [first value] by [date].

**Renewal / anniversary outreach (proactive — reach out BEFORE the date)**
> Hi [NAME], your [policy/contract/agreement] renews on [DATE]. Before it does, I want to make sure
> you're still getting the best [coverage/rate/terms] — a lot can change in a year. Do you have 15
> minutes this week to review? *(Insurance: re-shop at rate hikes. Freight: confirm lanes/volume.
> Real estate: tie to the next life-event window.)*

**Quarterly business review (QBR) request (larger accounts)**
> Hi [NAME], we're coming up on [quarter/milestone]. I'd like to walk you through the results so far
> — [metric/outcome] — and map out [next period]. Worth 30 minutes? I'll send a short agenda.

**Cross-sell / account rounding (expansion — from a position of trust)**
> [NAME], since we're already handling [current product/line], I noticed [gap/opportunity, e.g. "you
> don't have [X] with us"]. Clients who combine [A] and [B] usually [benefit — better rate/retention/
> coverage/coverage of more lanes]. Want me to put together a quick look?
