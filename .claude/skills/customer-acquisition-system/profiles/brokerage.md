# Industry Profile — Brokerage (cold-outreach-heavy)

> A worked profile for a brokerage whose primary motion is **cold outreach**. "Brokerage" spans
> several regulated types (freight/logistics, real estate, mortgage, insurance, business/M&A,
> securities) — the funnel mechanics are shared, but the **compliance overlay differs**, so the
> first thing to pin down is *which brokerage this is*. Fill the `[BRACKETS]` for the specific
> venture; treat everything as a starting configuration to tune against real data.

## 1. Snapshot
- **Business type:** [freight / real estate / mortgage / insurance / business / securities] brokerage
- **What's sold / to whom:** brokerage service (match + facilitate a deal) → [buyer role, e.g.
  shippers, sellers, borrowers, business owners]
- **Deal size / cycle:** [per-deal commission/spread] · [cycle — often weeks; relationship-led]
- **Dominant motion:** **outbound cold** + referral. Brokerage is trust- and relationship-heavy,
  so the phone leads and referrals compound (see `relational-acquisition.md`).

## 2. ICP & targeting
- **Ideal customer:** [segment] with recurring need and a real switching pain — e.g. shippers with
  steady lanes; owners near a sale/refi/renewal; businesses at a transaction trigger.
- **Disqualifiers:** one-off/price-only buyers with no repeat value; outside licensed geography.
- **Buying triggers:** contract/policy **renewal or lapse dates**, rate changes, a bad experience
  with the incumbent, growth/expansion, seasonality, regulatory deadlines.
- **List sources:** public registries/licensing databases, association & association-member lists,
  data providers, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, event/trade-show lists, and **referrals** (highest
  quality for brokerage). Verify phone type (mobile vs landline) — matters for TCPA.

## 3. Offer & hook
- **Core problem (Anxiety):** the counterparty is leaving money/time/risk on the table with their
  current arrangement (bad rates, poor service, no options, doing it themselves).
- **Specific outcome/proof:** [e.g. "found [client] [X]% better rates / closed in [Y] days"] — use
  real, disclosable numbers and names where regulation permits.
- **Low-risk first ask:** a free **quote / market check / valuation / rate benchmark** — brokerages
  have a natural no-obligation first step; lean on it.
- **Differentiator:** access/relationships/options the prospect can't easily get alone, plus
  white-glove handling. Sell the *broker's* value, not just price.

## 4. Channel mix & priority
1. **Cold call** (primary — brokerage is a phone business; trust is built by voice).
2. **Cold email** (to open doors and follow up; deliverability infra mandatory).
3. **LinkedIn/social** (warm the relationship, especially B2B brokerage).
4. **SMS** (WARM/consented only — after opt-in; strong for renewal reminders and speed-to-lead).
5. **Referrals & partners** (compounding — see §9).
- **Cadence depth:** ~6–8 touches over ~2–3 weeks, multi-channel, per the follow-up engine.
- **Speed-to-lead:** call inbound/quote requests within ~5 minutes; same-day on any reply.

## 5. Compliance regime — DO NOT SKIP (see compliance.md)
- **Outreach law:** TCPA + National/state **DNC scrubbing** for calls; **CAN-SPAM** for email;
  **10DLC + consent** for any SMS; GDPR/CASL if contacting EU/UK/Canada.
- **Licensing overlay (pick yours):** freight → FMCSA broker authority (MC#) + BMC-84 bond; real
  estate → state RE license + agency disclosures + RESPA; mortgage → NMLS/SAFE Act + TILA/RESPA;
  insurance → state producer license + advertising rules; securities → SEC/FINRA + Reg BI +
  cold-contact/recordkeeping rules.
- **Referral fees are frequently regulated** in these fields (e.g. RESPA) — clear the referral
  program (§9) and the scripts with counsel before using.
- **Gate:** counsel sign-off on the outreach program **before** scaling dials/sends.

## 6. Qualification rubric
- **Qualified =** real recurring need + within licensed scope + decision-maker reachable +
  timeline within [this quarter] + able to transact. Score Fit × Intent (see `closing.md`).
- **Track lost reasons:** price, stayed with incumbent, timing, trust, not qualified, no response.

## 7. Objection set (build responses in scripts-library.md)
- "I already have a broker/agent." · "Just tell me your rate/price." · "How do I know I can trust
  you?" · "I'll do it myself." · "Not the right time." · "What's your fee / who pays you?"

## 8. Economics & KPIs
- **Acquisition ceiling / target CAC:** [set from per-deal commission — CAC well under one deal's
  contribution margin].
- **CAC payback:** [target months]; brokerage repeat/renewal revenue makes payback favorable if
  retention holds.
- **LTV basis:** contribution margin across the **repeat/renewal** relationship, not a single deal.
- **Funnel benchmarks to watch:** dials→connect, connect→meeting, meeting→deal, quote→bind/close,
  and **repeat/referral rate** (the brokerage flywheel).

## 9. Supply/partner side (the referral flywheel)
- **Partner nodes:** existing clients, complementary professionals (e.g. for freight: carriers,
  dispatchers; real estate: lenders, attorneys, contractors; insurance: agents in other lines),
  and community/association contacts.
- **Payout design:** retention-/deal-weighted, staggered (see `relational-acquisition.md`) — and
  **compliant** with referral-fee rules for the brokerage type.

## 10. Tech stack
- **CRM** (with the measurement + follow-up fields), **power/parallel dialer**, **email
  sequencer** with separate authenticated domains, **enrichment/verification**, **10DLC-registered
  SMS**, **e-sign**. AI drafts and enriches; humans make every relationship call (see
  `ai-governance.md`).

## 11. Retention & expansion
- **First value:** the first successful brokered deal handled flawlessly — this is what earns the
  renewal and the referral.
- **Expansion/reactivation:** track renewal/lapse dates and reach out ahead of them; run a
  reactivation cadence on dormant clients and old closed-lost quotes.

## 12. Capacity check
- **Constraint:** a broker's throughput is personal attention and deal-handling capacity. Winning
  more counterparties than you can service well destroys trust — the core asset of a brokerage.
- **Scale gate:** grow dials/sends only when deal-handling and service capacity can absorb the wins
  (the operating rule in `SKILL.md`).
