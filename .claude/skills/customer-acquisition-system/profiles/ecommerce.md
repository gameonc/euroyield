# Profile — E-commerce / Online Store (marketing-led, self-serve)

> A ready profile for acquiring customers online where **the store and automated flows do the
> closing**, not a human rep. This is the clearest proof the system runs anywhere you need
> clientele: same backbone (capture → attribution → nurture → retention → dashboards), different
> motion. Fill the `[BRACKETS]` per store. Verify compliance specifics with counsel.

## Model difference (read first)

E-commerce is **marketing-led / self-serve**: higher volume, lower touch, and the "close" happens
at checkout, not on a call. So the emphasis shifts:
- The **closing toolkit** (`closing.md`) is largely replaced by **conversion-rate optimization
  (CRO)** on the store + **automated email/SMS flows**.
- **Speed-to-lead** becomes **abandoned-cart recovery timing** and the **welcome flow**.
- The **Lead Engine** (`lead-engine.md`), **lifecycle** (`lifecycle.md`), and **pricing**
  (`pricing.md`) apply almost directly — they're the heart of ecom.

For high-consideration / high-ticket ecom (furniture, B2B, custom), a human still closes — blend in
`closing.md` and `follow-up.md`. Tune the mix to ticket size and consideration.

## 1. Snapshot
- **Customer:** online shoppers in [niche]; **"supply" = product inventory**; paid via **product
  contribution margin per order**.
- **Motion:** marketing-led, self-serve, high-volume; the funnel closes, not a rep.

## 2. ICP & targeting
- **Ideal customer:** [persona — who buys, why, price sensitivity].
- **Triggers:** need/desire, seasonality, retargeting after a visit, restock/replenishment.
- **Traffic sources:** paid social (Meta/TikTok), Google Search + Shopping/PMax, SEO/content,
  influencer/UGC, and the **owned email/SMS list** (the compounding asset).

## 3. Offer & hook
- **Hero offer:** [the product + its hook]; bundles, free shipping threshold, first-order discount.
- **Risk-reversal:** returns/guarantee, reviews/social proof (the ecom trust engine).
- **Low-risk first step:** email/SMS capture (pop-up + welcome offer) — capture the visitor even if
  they don't buy today, so the nurture/flows can work.

## 4. Channel mix & priority
1. **Paid social** (Meta/TikTok — creative-led; UGC wins).
2. **Paid search / Shopping / PMax** (high intent).
3. **Owned email/SMS** (welcome, browse/cart abandonment, post-purchase, win-back) — highest ROI.
4. **SEO/content** + **influencer/UGC/affiliate**.
5. **Retargeting** across the above.
- "Speed-to-lead" = fire the **welcome flow** and **cart-abandonment flow** within minutes/hours.

## 5. Compliance regime (verify — see compliance.md)
- **Email:** CAN-SPAM (+ GDPR/CASL if applicable). **SMS:** consent + 10DLC.
- **Ads:** platform policies; **FTC** truth-in-advertising and **endorsement/UGC disclosure** rules
  (influencers must disclose). **Privacy:** CCPA/CPRA, cookie consent, data handling.

## 6. Qualification / segmentation
- Little human qualification — instead **segment by behavior** (browsed, added-to-cart, purchased,
  lapsed) and score/prioritize with `lead_score`. Route each segment to the right flow.

## 7. On-site objections (handled by CRO + copy, not a rep)
- Price, shipping cost, trust/legitimacy, fit/sizing, returns, "will it actually work" → answered
  with reviews, guarantees, clear shipping/returns, sizing guides, UGC, and fast support.

## 8. Economics & KPIs
- **Contribution margin per order** (net of COGS, shipping, fees, discounts) — the true basis for
  CAC and LTV.
- **KPIs:** conversion rate, **AOV**, **CPA/CAC**, **ROAS**, **cart-abandonment rate**, **repeat
  purchase rate**, LTV, **LTV:CAC**. (Maps directly to `lead-engine.md` dashboards + `measurement.md`.)

## 9. Supply/partner side (the ecom referral engine)
- **Influencers / UGC creators / affiliates** are the partner engine — run an affiliate/creator
  program with retention/performance-weighted payouts (`relational-acquisition.md`), plus a
  customer **referral program** ("give $X, get $X").

## 10. Tools
- **Store:** Shopify (this session has a Shopify connection). **Email/SMS flows:** Klaviyo (or
  similar). **Ads:** Meta/Google. **Reviews/UGC:** Okendo/Yotpo. **Analytics/attribution:** GA4 +
  a blended-ROAS tool (e.g. Triple Whale). AI drafts creative/flows; humans own brand + offers
  (`ai-governance.md`).

## 11. Retention & expansion (where ecom profit really lives)
- **Post-purchase flows**, **subscriptions/replenishment**, **cross-sell/upsell**, **loyalty**, and
  **win-back** (cart-abandonment + lapsed-purchaser flows) — see `lifecycle.md`. First-order CAC is
  often recovered only on the **2nd+ order**, so repeat rate is the whole game.

## 12. Capacity check
- **Constraint:** inventory/fulfillment/shipping + customer-support capacity. Don't scale ad spend
  past the ability to fulfill and support orders — stockouts and slow shipping burn the brand (same
  operating rule as everywhere else).
