# Vertical Overlay — Insurance Brokerage

> Inherits `base.md`. States what's specific to an insurance brokerage/agency; the rest defaults to
> base. **Verify all regulatory specifics with counsel — licensing, rebating, and replacement rules
> vary by state (see `compliance.md`).**

## 1. Snapshot
- **Customer:** policyholders (personal lines — auto/home/life; and/or commercial lines).
- **Supply side:** insurance carriers you're **appointed** with.
- **How you get paid:** **commission from carriers — new-business commission plus recurring
  renewal/residual commission**, sometimes contingent/bonus for volume & loss ratio.
- **Deal nature:** recurring by design — policies **renew annually**. Retention compounds income.

## 2. ICP & targeting
- **Ideal customer:** clients with **multi-policy potential** (home + auto + umbrella; a business
  with several lines) and reasonable risk profile — the ones worth rounding out and retaining.
- **Buying triggers:** **policy renewal/anniversary dates**, **rate increases from their current
  carrier** (the #1 switch trigger), life events (marriage, new home, new car, new baby, new
  business), business growth/new assets.
- **List sources:** referrals + **your existing book** (cross-sell), COIs (realtors, mortgage
  officers, CPAs, auto dealers), commercial cold outreach, online quote leads.

## 3. Offer & hook
- **Problem (Anxiety):** overpaying, being underinsured, coverage gaps, a rate hike, confusing policies.
- **Outcome/proof:** better coverage/price by shopping multiple carriers; expert advocacy at claim
  time; a specific proof ("saved [client] $X while improving coverage").
- **Low-risk first ask:** free **quote / policy review / coverage comparison** — the natural insurance
  foot-in-door (especially near a renewal or after a rate hike).
- **Differentiator:** access to **multiple carriers** (vs. a captive agent), independent advice, and
  claims advocacy.

## 4. Channel mix & priority
1. **Referrals + cross-sell the existing book** (primary — cheapest, highest-converting).
2. **Phone** (quotes, reviews, commercial outreach).
3. **COIs** (realtors/mortgage/CPAs — recurring referral streams).
4. **Online quote leads** (speed-to-lead critical) and commercial cold outreach.
- Time outreach to **renewal windows** — reaching someone ~30–60 days before their anniversary or
  right after a rate hike beats cold timing.

## 5. Compliance regime (verify — see compliance.md)
- **Licensing:** state **producer license** (per line/state), **carrier appointments**, **E&O
  insurance**.
- **Conduct rules:** **anti-rebating** laws (limits on giving value to induce purchase — varies by
  state and is loosening in some), **replacement** regulations (esp. life/annuity), and advertising
  rules. **Referral compensation to unlicensed persons is restricted** — verify before designing
  referral incentives.
- **Outreach law:** TCPA/DNC (consumer calls — high scrutiny), CAN-SPAM, 10DLC for SMS.

## 6. Qualification
- **Qualified =** insurable risk you can place + near a decision point (renewal/rate hike/life event)
  + multi-line or retention potential.
- **Lost reasons:** price, loyalty to current agent, "just wanted a quote", coverage confusion, non-standard risk.

## 7. Objection set
- "Just give me a price." · "I've had my agent forever." · "Insurance is all the same." ·
  "I don't have time." · "Why is this cheaper — what's the catch?"

## 8. Economics & KPIs
- **Commission = new + renewal**; the asset is the **renewing book**. LTV is multi-year renewal
  contribution margin, so **retention rate drives everything**.
- **KPIs:** quote→bind rate, **policies per client (PPC / account rounding)**, **retention/renewal
  rate**, loss ratio (affects contingent income), and referral rate.

## 9. Supply/partner side (COIs)
- **COIs** are the referral engine: realtors and mortgage officers (home/auto at closing), CPAs and
  attorneys (commercial), auto dealers. Build two-way, retention-weighted relationships — and keep
  referral compensation compliant with insurance law.

## 10. Tools
- **AMS (agency management system):** Applied Epic, EZLynx, AMS360, HawkSoft, or an agency CRM;
  **comparative raters**; dialer + sequencer for outreach; renewal/anniversary tracking.

## 11. Retention & expansion (the core of insurance economics)
- **Retention:** proactive **renewal management** (reach out before the anniversary, re-shop at rate
  hikes), reviews, claims advocacy. **Multi-line clients retain dramatically better than monoline** —
  so retention and expansion are the same motion (see `lifecycle.md`).
- **Expansion:** **account rounding** (add lines), umbrella/upsell, commercial cross-sell, life/health.

## 12. Capacity check
- **Constraint:** service/servicing bandwidth (endorsements, claims, renewals). A book that outgrows
  service capacity churns at renewal — the opposite of the goal. Gate new-business growth on service
  capacity so the renewing book stays healthy.
