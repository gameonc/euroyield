# Vertical Overlay — Real Estate Brokerage

> Inherits `base.md`. States what's specific to a real estate brokerage; the rest defaults to base.
> **Verify all regulatory specifics with counsel — the 2024 NAR settlement and RESPA rules are
> evolving (see `compliance.md`).**

## 1. Snapshot
- **Customer:** buyers and sellers (and, for a brokerage, the **agents** you recruit and retain).
- **Supply side:** inventory/listings and the MLS.
- **How you get paid:** **commission** on closed transactions (brokerage takes a split of agent
  commissions; models vary — graduated splits, caps, desk fees, 100%-plus-fee).
- **Deal nature:** infrequent per client (people transact every several years) but **high referral
  and repeat potential** across a lifetime.

## 2. ICP & targeting
- **Ideal customer:** buyers/sellers with a real, near-term move; for the brokerage side,
  **productive agents** to recruit. Farm a defined geography or niche (first-time buyers, luxury,
  investors, relocations).
- **Buying triggers:** **life events** (marriage, kids, divorce, death, job relocation, retirement),
  lease expirations, rate/equity changes, expired/withdrawn listings, FSBOs.
- **List sources:** **sphere of influence (SOI)** and past clients (highest quality), geographic
  farming, open houses, portal leads (Zillow/Realtor.com), expired/FSBO lists, social.

## 3. Offer & hook
- **Problem (Anxiety):** buying/selling is high-stakes, confusing, and expensive to get wrong.
- **Outcome/proof:** local expertise, pricing/negotiation results, smooth transaction; a specific
  proof (recent comparable sale, days-on-market, over-ask results).
- **Low-risk first ask:** free **home valuation / CMA**, or a buyer consultation.
- **Differentiator:** local market mastery, negotiation, network, and full-service handling.

## 4. Channel mix & priority
1. **SOI + past-client referrals** (primary — real estate runs on relationships and repeat/referral).
2. **Phone** (calls to SOI, leads, expireds/FSBOs).
3. **Geographic farming** (mail/door/social to a defined area).
4. **Portal leads** (Zillow/Realtor.com — pay-to-play, speed-to-lead critical).
5. **Social + content** (local authority).

## 5. Compliance regime (verify — see compliance.md)
- **Licensing:** state **real estate license**; brokerage supervision requirements; agency
  disclosures.
- **Referral fees:** **RESPA Section 8** prohibits kickbacks/fee-splitting for referrals of
  settlement services (mortgage, title, escrow); affiliated-business arrangements have strict rules.
  Real-estate-to-real-estate referral fees between licensees are generally allowed — **verify**.
- **2024 NAR settlement:** buyer-broker **written agreements** required before touring, and
  cooperative-compensation/commission practices changed (commissions negotiable, not set). *(confirm
  current implementation)*
- **Outreach law:** TCPA/DNC (calls to consumers — high scrutiny), CAN-SPAM, 10DLC for SMS.

## 6. Qualification
- **Qualified =** motivated + timeline within months + financially able (pre-approved buyer / real
  seller) + working with you (buyer-broker agreement).
- **Lost reasons:** commission, "waiting on the market", using a friend/family agent, not ready.

## 7. Objection set
- "Why is your commission that much?" · "I'll wait for rates/prices." · "I know an agent." ·
  "I'll just sell it myself (FSBO)." · "Zillow says my home is worth more."

## 8. Economics & KPIs
- **Per-deal commission** with brokerage split; watch **cost per lead vs. close rate** (portal leads
  can be expensive with low conversion).
- **KPIs:** lead→appointment→agreement→close, days-on-market, list-to-sale ratio, and — the real
  engine — **repeat + referral rate** and **database/SOI touches**.

## 9. Supply/partner side (COIs & agent recruiting)
- **Centers of influence:** lenders, title/escrow, attorneys, contractors, financial advisors —
  two-way referral relationships (mind RESPA on settlement-service referrals).
- **Agent recruiting** (for the brokerage): recruit/retain productive agents with retention-weighted
  value (splits, leads, support) — treat like the supply-side pipeline in `relational-acquisition.md`.

## 10. Tools
- **MLS**, real-estate **CRM** (Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, LionDesk, Sierra), portal lead platforms,
  transaction management (Dotloop/SkySlope), dialer for lead/expired follow-up.

## 11. Retention & expansion
- **Retention:** systematic **SOI nurture** — stay top-of-mind for years so you get the repeat deal
  and the referrals (this IS the business; see `lifecycle.md`).
- **Expansion:** repeat transactions, referrals, and adjacent services (referrals to partners within
  RESPA limits); for the brokerage, expanding the agent roster.

## 12. Capacity check
- **Constraint:** an agent's personal transaction bandwidth and responsiveness. Overloading leads to
  slow follow-up (fatal given speed-to-lead) and poor client experience. Gate lead-buying/growth on
  the ability to work leads fast and serve clients well.
