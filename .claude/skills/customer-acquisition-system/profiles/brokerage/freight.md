# Vertical Overlay — Freight / Logistics Brokerage

> Inherits `base.md`. This overlay states what's specific to a freight brokerage; anything not
> restated defaults to the base. **Verify all regulatory specifics with counsel — thresholds and
> rules change (see `compliance.md`).**

## 1. Snapshot
- **Customer:** shippers (their logistics/traffic managers, ops, owners).
- **Supply side:** carriers (owner-operators, fleets) sourced to cover loads.
- **How you get paid:** the **margin/spread** between the shipper rate and the carrier rate.
- **Deal nature:** recurring lanes and repeat freight — a relationship business, not one-off loads.

## 2. ICP & targeting
- **Ideal customer:** shippers with **consistent, repeatable lane volume** in your niche
  (reefer/flatbed/dry van/LTL/specialized) and geography; enough volume to matter, not so big
  you're one of 50 brokers.
- **Disqualifiers:** pure spot-market, lowest-price-only shippers with no loyalty or repeat value.
- **Buying triggers:** capacity crunches, a carrier/broker failing them (service failure, a missed
  pickup), seasonal surges, new lanes, a shipper's own growth.
- **List sources:** industry directories, shipper databases, port/industrial-park research,
  referrals, and reversing load-board activity. Verify contact phone type for TCPA.

## 3. Offer & hook
- **Problem (Anxiety):** unreliable capacity, service failures, freight that doesn't get covered,
  opaque pricing.
- **Outcome/proof:** reliable coverage on their lanes, communication, claims handling; a specific
  proof ("we've never missed a pickup on [lane type] for [client]").
- **Low-risk first ask:** quote a live/upcoming load or a lane rate — the natural freight foot-in-door.
- **Differentiator:** carrier relationships/capacity in their lanes, responsiveness, ownership of problems.

## 4. Channel mix & priority
1. **Cold-call shippers** (primary — freight is a phone business).
2. **Email** (intro + follow-up; deliverability infra required).
3. **LinkedIn** (logistics managers, warm-up).
4. **Referrals** (from happy shippers and carriers).
- Note: **load boards (DAT, Truckstop.com)** are for **carrier sourcing / covering freight**, not
  customer acquisition — don't confuse the supply channel with the demand channel.
- Cadence & speed-to-lead per base.

## 5. Compliance regime (verify — see compliance.md)
- **Broker authority:** FMCSA **broker authority (MC number)**, **BMC-84 surety bond (commonly
  ~$75,000)**, BOC-3 process agents. *(confirm current amounts/requirements)*
- **Carrier vetting is a compliance + liability function:** verify carrier authority, insurance, and
  safety (FMCSA/SAFER) before tendering — tools like MyCarrierPackets, Highway, RMIS, Carrier411.
- **Outreach law:** TCPA/DNC for cold calls, CAN-SPAM for email, 10DLC for any SMS (base + compliance.md).
- **Contracts:** broker-carrier and broker-shipper agreements; cargo/contingent cargo considerations.

## 6. Qualification
- **Qualified =** real recurring lane volume in a niche you can cover + reachable decision-maker +
  willingness to give a broker a shot (a test lane) + within authority.
- **Lost reasons to track:** price, incumbent broker, "we go direct to carriers", trust/claims, timing.

## 7. Objection set (build responses in scripts-library.md)
- "I already have brokers." · "Just give me your rate." · "Can you actually cover my lanes?" ·
  "We contract carriers direct." · "Had a bad experience with a broker before."

## 8. Economics & KPIs
- **Margin discipline:** track margin per load and per lane; CAC well under the contribution margin
  of a shipper's repeat freight.
- **KPIs:** shipper acquisition cost, **loads/shipper**, margin %, on-time %, claims rate, and
  **repeat-lane / retention rate** (the freight flywheel).

## 9. Supply/partner side
- Carriers are the **supply**, not the customer — but treat top carriers as partners (they enable
  reliability and can refer shippers). Carrier retention supports customer retention.

## 10. Tools
- **TMS** (transportation management system) as the operational hub; DAT/Truckstop for capacity;
  carrier-vetting tool; CRM for shipper pipeline; dialer + email sequencer for outreach.

## 11. Retention & expansion
- **Retention:** consistent capacity + proactive communication → the shipper keeps tendering. One
  service failure can lose the account — service *is* retention here.
- **Expansion:** more lanes, more modes, higher share of the shipper's freight (lane/volume growth).

## 12. Capacity check
- **Constraint:** carrier capacity + your team's load-handling bandwidth. Don't sign shippers whose
  freight you can't reliably cover — a missed load costs the relationship. Gate growth on coverage
  capacity (base + operating rule).
