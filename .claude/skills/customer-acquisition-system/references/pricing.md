# Pricing Posture (Phase 4, value capture)

Pricing is where willingness-to-pay is realized. The strongest evidence for aggressive
segmentation is field-experimental: in one large **digital** setting, exercising previously
unused market power raised profit by ~55%, and personalized pricing then added ~19% over the
optimized uniform price (and ~86% over the non-optimized baseline). Notably, **>60% of
consumers benefited** from personalization even though aggregate consumer surplus fell.

Two cautions that govern how to use this:
- The result is strong on **internal validity** in that setting; **generalizability** to other
  industries — especially trust-sensitive B2B and relationship-led services — is uncertain.
- It is **not** a license to deploy algorithmic pricing everywhere. Trust and reputational risk
  can swamp the theoretical gain.

## The pricing maturity ladder

Move up deliberately; do not skip to the top by default. In trust-sensitive or relationship-led
businesses, progress uniform → segmented → negotiated, and only reach individualized pricing
once governance is mature.

| Posture | Best use | Evidence signal | Control requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Uniform list pricing | Simple offers, low complexity, early-stage consistency | Highest trust, lowest precision | Low |
| Segmented / tiered | Distinct needs by size, service level, urgency | Strong practical fit for B2B and services | Medium |
| Negotiated value-based | Custom or high-ticket contracts | Best when value is heterogeneous and provable | Medium–high |
| Algorithmic personalization | Large-scale digital demand | Strong in the studied digital context, uncertain elsewhere | High |

## How to advise

- Review every step up the ladder through two lenses: **cost of errors** and **type of
  knowledge required** (see `ai-governance.md`), plus privacy/security controls.
- Segmented and negotiated pricing capture most of the realizable upside for services and B2B
  without the trust risk of personalization.
- Reserve algorithmic personalization for high-volume digital demand environments **with mature
  governance** — and even then, watch reputational exposure, not just the profit delta.
