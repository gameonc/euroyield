# Relational Acquisition (Phase 4)

For high-trust, high-friction, operationally complex businesses, prioritize direct,
relationship-driven channels. They give the most control over qualification, objection
handling, pricing, and the handoff to delivery. Lower-touch channels (marketplaces, paid
social) still matter, but they are weakest exactly where these businesses are most fragile:
trust and fulfillment control.

Directionally, in-person and outbound relationship channels tend to outperform low-touch
channels for small businesses — but treat channel-share survey figures as *market context,
not proof* (see `evidence-base.md`; the underlying survey methodology is often undisclosed).

## Outreach: the A-I-M cadence

A-I-M is a practitioner sales methodology (label it as such — it is not peer-reviewed research).
It moves prospecting from product-first pitching toward low-friction, value-led engagement:

- **A — Anxiety:** the business problem or risk that creates urgency. Lead with the problem, not the product.
- **I — Influence:** the internal and external forces shaping the buyer's decision (stakeholders, incumbents, timing pressure).
- **M — Motivation:** the concrete business outcome that would justify change.

Structure discovery and messaging around these three before ever describing features.

## Referral program design

Referred customers tend to have **higher contribution margins, higher retention, and
materially higher lifetime value** than comparable non-referred customers (large-bank study:
at least ~16% greater value). The correct takeaway is **not** "pay everyone to refer";
it is **target high-quality advocates and pay for durable performance.**

Four design components:

1. **Trigger the ask at objectively positive moments** — successful delivery, milestone usage,
   post-onboarding satisfaction, a renewal.
2. **Make incentives two-sided** — advocate and new customer both benefit; this lowers social
   friction and improves conversion quality.
3. **Stagger payouts by retention thresholds** — do not pay it all upfront; pay as the referred
   customer proves durable.
4. **Tag every referral by source** so cohort quality is measurable over time.

### Recommended payout logic (a design, tune to the venture)

| Milestone | Advocate reward | New-customer reward | Release condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qualified referral accepted | $0–$50 token value | Optional waived fee / setup benefit | Referral valid and contacted |
| New customer activates | $100 | $100 credit or service value | First transaction / start date confirmed |
| Retained at 90 days | $150 | None or small upgrade | No churn or major delinquency |
| Retained at 180 days | $250 | None or loyalty benefit | Still active and serviceable |
| High-value expansion | Variable bonus | None | Upsell / second-purchase threshold reached |

## Supply-side / partner recruiting pipeline

Same retention-weighted logic applies to recruiting a supply side (drivers, installers,
clinicians, hosts, resellers). The highest-yield partners are **nodes of trust and repeated
contact**, not passive job boards. Reward retention, not sign-up volume — because technology
or cash incentives alone don't guarantee adoption without implementation support.

Worked example — **driver-recruiting pipeline** (translate the partner types to your venture):

| Partner | Primary role | KPI | Suggested economics |
|---|---|---|---|
| CDL / trade school | Candidate pipeline | Qualified graduates interviewed | Per-hire bounty *after activation* |
| Repair shop / local hub | Local trust node | Referred candidates/month | Small lead fee + retention bonus |
| Insurance agent | Quality filter | Insurance-ready candidate rate | Shared bonus at 90/180 days |
| Dispatcher / compliance advisor | Capability screen | Ready-to-work % | Bonus only if candidate clears onboarding and stays active |
| Existing driver | Social proof + fit screen | Referral-to-activation rate | Tiered milestone bonus |
| Community moderator | Audience access | Cost per qualified applicant | Sponsored content / community fee, not per click |

**Conservative sample payout schedule** (protects cash, aligns with retention):
$250 after 30 active days, $250 after 90 active days, $500 after 180 active days, plus an
optional bonus only if the recruit hits a revenue/utilization threshold. Front-loaded
bounties leak cash and reward churn; staggered payouts turn recruiting into customer-capital
formation.
